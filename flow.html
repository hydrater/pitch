Proprietary Signal System

What is Flow?

Flow is our proprietary metric framework that quantifies institutional hedging activity in the derivatives market. It transforms raw market data into four distinct signals that measure the level, speed, depth, and acceleration of institutional positioning.

Institutional traders operating at scale are forced to hedge through derivatives. These purchases leave a measurable footprint. Flow captures that footprint across four dimensions, giving us a real-time view of what large players are doing before it shows up in price.

Core Thesis

Why Large Traders Leave Traces

Institutions managing billions cannot move in and out of positions quickly without moving the market against themselves. They are forced to hedge through derivatives, and those purchases are recorded.

🏦

Proprietary Metrics

Four Dimensions of Flow

Each metric captures a different aspect of institutional behavior. Used together, they form a multi-dimensional view of market intent.

Level

Flow Index

Aggregate Hedging Pressure

Measures the aggregate cost of portfolio insurance across the derivatives market. A high reading means institutions are paying a premium to hedge because they expect turbulence. A low reading means complacency, with little demand for protection.

High reading Fear / active hedging
Low reading Complacency / no protection
Momentum

Flow Momentum

Rate of Change in Hedging

Captures the speed at which institutions are buying protection. A sharp increase means sudden demand for hedging, often triggered by new information or a large player entering the market.

Rapid increase Panic buying of protection
Rapid decrease Fear subsiding, hedges unwinding
Meta-Level

Flow Depth

Second-Order Hedging Signal

When institutions hedge their hedges, this metric rises. It captures extreme uncertainty, revealing moments when even the volatility market itself is unstable and indicating deep institutional stress.

Elevated Hedging demand on the hedges
Stable Orderly volatility markets
Acceleration

Flow Acceleration

Rate of Change in Flow Depth

The acceleration of hedging activity. A spike signals a sudden escalation in institutional stress, often serving as the earliest warning of a regime shift before the primary Flow Index fully reacts.

Spiking Regime shift underway
Stable No change in hedging behavior

Regime Detection

Flow Index and Flow Momentum are combined into an effective score, amplified by Flow Depth during extreme conditions. This composite signal classifies the market into distinct regimes, each triggering a different portfolio allocation between growth and defensive assets.

Contraction Recovery

After a spike, the Flow Index contracting from its recent high signals that hedging demand is subsiding and institutional selling pressure is easing. Strategies detect meaningful drops from rolling peaks to enter recovery positions ahead of broader market recognition.

Flow Momentum Flow Index
📊

Volatility-Scaled Exposure

Position sizes are adjusted inversely to the current Flow Index level. When institutional hedging is heavy, exposure is automatically reduced. In calm markets with low hedging demand, the system stays fully invested. This creates a natural risk-off mechanism driven by institutional behavior rather than arbitrary rules.

Structural Advantage

Why This Edge Persists

💡

Intent Over Price

Price tells you what happened. Derivatives tell you what institutions expect to happen. By reading hedging flows, we access forward-looking information that price-based analysis misses entirely.

🌊

Unavoidable Footprint

Large players cannot hide in the derivatives market. The volume required to hedge billions in positions inevitably moves implied volatility. This structural constraint makes the signal persistent and reliable across market regimes.

🧩

Compounding Signals

A single reading is a data point. The combination of level, rate of change, and second-order effects creates a multi-dimensional view of institutional behavior that is exponentially harder to extract than any one signal alone.
